Why traditional computer-based testing fails and how game-induced arousal enhances retention.

❌ Traditional 2D CBT Limitations

  • Passive multiple-choice heuristic guessing without cognitive commitment
  • High monotony and attention depletion over extended study sessions
  • Lack of spatial-temporal tension or emotional engagement
  • "Chocolate-covered broccoli" gamification (static badges and timer bars)

✅ AeroQuiz Flight-Integrated Learning

  • Spatial Decision Commitment: Players must fly toward and physically shoot target pylons
  • Flow State Induction: Balancing altitude control, banking, and answer evaluation
  • Yerkes-Dodson Law: Mild benign flight stress sharpens neural memory retrieval
  • Immediate Visceral Feedback: Explosion of correct targets vs airframe damage smoke

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATIONS

Aerodynamic Banking & Hermite Camera Smoothing

Rigorous mathematics ensuring smooth flight without simulator disorientation.

Dynamic Roll Banking Formulation

Banking angle is coupled to instantaneous lateral steering velocity:

θ_target = -clamp(vx / vmax, -1.0, 1.0) * (π / 5) θ(t + Δt) = θ(t) + (θ_target - θ(t)) * (1 - e^(-k * Δt))

Prevents jerky turns and simulates real aircraft inertia.

Cubic Smoothstep Camera Interpolation

Smooth Hermite damping eliminates rapid camera jerking:

S(τ) = 3τ² - 2τ³, τ = clamp(Δt / T_lag, 0, 1) P_cam(t + Δt) = P_cam(t) + S(τ) * (P_plane + o_cam - P_cam(t))

Maintains depression angle (-8.5°) for horizon and crosshair clarity.

VISUAL ACUITY & OPTICAL DESIGN

Dynamic Spawning Geometry & High-Contrast Readability

Ensuring optimal reading time and zero sun-glare washouts.

Dynamic Gate Spawner

Calculates spawn depth based on forward velocity:

Z_spawn = Z_plane + vz * t_decision + buffer ≈ Z_plane + 260m

Guarantees ~6.8 seconds of evaluation window regardless of speed.

Atmospheric Shielding

  • High-saturation solid color backing boxes
  • Zero specular highlights to prevent sun flare
  • Distinct colors for all 4 options (Blue, Amber, Green, Purple)

Dual-Pass Outlines

  • BitmapText text nodes with 4-offset black shadow pass
  • Exceeds 12:1 contrast ratio against bright skies
  • Lowered flight horizon avoids ground occlusion

  • 2 Lives (Damaged): Trailing gray smoke plume (25 particles/sec)
  • 1 Life (Critical): Heavy billowing black smoke + fire bursts (65 p/s)

Avionics Sound & Pitch Shifting
